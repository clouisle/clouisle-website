v0.2.0 lays the foundation of Clouisle as a multi-agent collaboration platform.

## Teams and permissions

The platform ships with organization-level team isolation and an RBAC role system.

![Team management](https://rustfs-endpoint.yhnotes.com/content/team-management.png)

## Knowledge bases

Knowledge bases support document chunking, hybrid vector search, and reranking, so agents answer with more relevant context.

## Models and tools

- Unified management for large language models
- Extensible tooling through the Model Context Protocol (MCP)

## Private deployment and security

Private deployment guardrails and SSO help organizations keep AI inside infrastructure they control.
